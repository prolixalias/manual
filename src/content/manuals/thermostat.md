## Sensors

There are three motion + temperature sensors:

- Living room, near the cold air return
- Upstairs master bedroom, on top of the tall mirror
- Basement "bar" area

With these, the thermostat knows which rooms are occupied and controls airflow accordingly.

<img src="/media/sensor.png" alt="Occupancy and temperature sensor" width="280" />

If a motion sensor bugs you, it's fine to turn it toward the wall for privacy — just let us know so we can point it back where it belongs.
