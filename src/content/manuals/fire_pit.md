Firewood may be obtained at many locations in and around Cheboygan. We can recommend spots if you're not having luck.

A kindling bucket (metal bucket inside the garage) is there for you to use. There's an apparatus (also in the garage near the bucket and a small sledge hammer) to split wood into kindling-size pieces. Just say the word if you're not comfortable using it.
